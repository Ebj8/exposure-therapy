# Privacy Policy

**Exposure Therapy** · by DataThink · Effective November 22, 2026

Exposure Therapy helps you clean up your iPhone's photo library. It was built so that **nothing leaves your iPhone**. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

- We don't collect any data. None.
- There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- Your photos are only ever shown and changed on your own device, and only when you ask.

## Your photos

To work, Exposure Therapy asks for access to your photo library. With your permission, it:

- **Shows** your photos so you can review them.
- **Marks a photo as a Favorite** when you heart it, and **adds a photo to an album** when you choose one.
- **Deletes photos** only when you tap **Breakup** and then confirm iOS's own "Delete photos?" prompt. Deleted photos go to the **Recently Deleted** album in the Photos app, where you can restore them for 30 days.

Photos are never uploaded, copied off your device or shared with us or anyone else. If you use iCloud Photos, your iPhone may download a photo from iCloud so it can be shown. That happens through Apple's Photos service, under Apple's privacy policy, not through us.

You can change or remove photo access at any time in **Settings → Apps → Exposure Therapy → Photos**.

## What the app stores on your iPhone

To remember your progress, the app keeps a small amount of information **on your device only**:

- Which photos you've reviewed and what you decided (kept, favorited, added to an album or marked for deletion), identified by Apple's internal photo IDs, not the photos themselves.
- Your stats, such as your streak and the storage you've freed.
- Your settings, such as your daily goal and reminder time.

This information never leaves your iPhone, except as part of your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which are managed by Apple. You can clear it at any time in the app under **Settings → Reset progress**, and it's removed completely if you delete the app.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications **on your iPhone**. No notification service, server or account is involved.

## Children

Exposure Therapy is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, any change would be to make it clearer, not to start collecting.

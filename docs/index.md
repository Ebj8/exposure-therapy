# Exposure Therapy

Break up with the photos you don't need. Exposure Therapy lets you swipe through your camera roll one month at a time: swipe to keep, swipe up to break up. Nothing is deleted until you say so, and nothing ever leaves your iPhone.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do my deleted photos go?**
When you tap **Breakup** and confirm, your exes move to the **Recently Deleted** album in the Photos app. They stay there for 30 days before iOS removes them for good.

**I deleted a photo by mistake. Can I get it back?**
Yes, for 30 days. Open the **Photos** app, go to **Albums → Recently Deleted**, select the photo and tap **Recover**.

**Does swiping up delete a photo right away?**
No. Swiping up marks it as an ex. You can change your mind with **Undo**, or by tapping it on the **Your exes** screen, any time before you tap **Breakup**. iOS also asks you to confirm before anything is deleted.

**What happens when I swipe past a photo?**
Swiping left or right off a photo you haven't decided on keeps it. Kept photos are never changed.

**The app only shows some of my photos.**
You may have given it access to only some photos. Go to **Settings → Apps → Exposure Therapy → Photos** and choose **Full Access**.

**How do I start over?**
In the app, go to **Settings → Reset progress**. This clears your decisions and stats only. It never touches your photos.

**Does the app upload my photos or collect data?**
No. There are no accounts, servers, analytics or ads. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

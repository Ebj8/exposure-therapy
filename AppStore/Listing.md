## Description (4,000 max)

Your camera roll is full of photos you'll never look at again: blurry shots, near-duplicates, screenshots from 2019. Exposure Therapy helps you finally let them go, one month at a time.

SWIPE THROUGH YOUR PHOTOS LIKE A GALLERY
Pick a month and browse it like the Photos app. Swipe left or right to move on, and every photo you swipe past is kept. Swipe up to break up with a photo you don't want. It leaves the gallery and waits on your exes list.

SEE THE WHOLE STORY
A filmstrip under each photo shows the shots taken just before and after it, so you can keep the best of a burst and let the rest go. Photos appear at their original shape, with nothing cropped.

NOTHING IS DELETED UNTIL YOU SAY SO
When you're done, the Your exes screen shows everything you've broken up with. Tap any photo to take it back. Hit Breakup when you're ready, and iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for 30 days.

MORE THAN KEEP OR DELETE
• Heart a photo to add it to your Favorites
• File a photo straight into an album
• Undo any decision, even after closing the app
• Change your mind about photos from earlier sessions

BUILD A HABIT
• A daily session of 25, 50 or 100 photos, with confetti when you hit your goal
• Streaks, storage freed and how much of your library you've reviewed
• An optional daily reminder at the time you choose
• Month-by-month progress so you always know where you left off

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads. Your photos never leave your iPhone, and the app collects no data at all.

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** No.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

> Exposure Therapy helps people review their photo library month by month and delete the photos they don't want. It requires access to the photo library: please choose "Allow Full Access" when prompted (a simulator or device with a few photos works).
>
> How to test: on Home, tap a month tile (or "Start swiping"). Swipe left/right to browse; photos you swipe past are kept. Swipe up to mark a photo for deletion ("break up"). Tap "Done" or swipe past the last photo to reach "Your exes", where photos can be rescued by tapping them. Tapping "Breakup" asks iOS to delete the remaining exes; iOS shows its own confirmation, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. Nothing is deleted before that confirmation.
>
> Other features: heart (marks a Favorite in Photos), add to album, undo, and an optional daily reminder (local notifications only). The app has no accounts, no network features and collects no data.
